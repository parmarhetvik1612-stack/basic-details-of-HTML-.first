Food processing sector to attract 1.02lakh cr investment </br>
New delhi: the goverment has signed agreements with 26 overseas and domestic firms during the “ world food india “ summit for investments worth rs. 1.02 lakh cr in india’s food processing sector.

The summit organised by the ministry of food processing industries, was held at bharat mandapan in the national capital ffrom september 25 to 28.

It an official statement on Sunday , the ministry said the world food india 2025 conclued on a historic note with investment commitments of unprecedented scale .
“

Over the cource of the four day event 26 leading domestic and global companies signed memoranda of under standing worth a total marketing of 102046.89 crore, marking one of the largest investment announcements in india’s food processing sector ,” the ministry added.


On the first day of summit , inaugurated by the prime minister chirag paswan had asserted that MOUS worth over Rs. 1 lakh crore would be signed during the four day event.
“these MOUS are projected to generate direct employment for 64k people and create indirect opprtunities for more than 10 lakh individuals”

The companies that signed realiance consumer products , the coca-cola on india , gujarat cooperative milk marketing federation , fair exports pvt ltd , nestle india , tata consumer products ltd and carlsberg india pvt ltd .
